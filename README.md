## About
Welcome! I am PhD scientist conducting research in privacy, usability, and extended reality (XR). My research interest involves studying the behavior of new sensing modalities, especially as they contribute to the user experience in emerging platforms like XR. I am proficient in research study design, data analysis, project management, and programming.

## Most Recent Projects
### Eye Movement Privacy Research
Identify and implement opportunities to enhance user privacy for eye tracking-enabled platforms.
### Eye Movement Data Collection
Using Unity, I design data collection protocols that elicit and collect a variety of eye movement in virtual/augmented reality devices. APIs provided by the manufacturers of such devices are not designed for research applications; I re-engineer these APIs to meet our needs. Compatible platforms include the Meta Quest Pro, Microsoft HoloLens 2, PupilLabs, FOVE, Vive, and SensoMotoric Instruments. 
### Benchmarking Signal Quality for Eye Movement in Novel Platforms
Using R, MATLAB, and Python, I characterize eye movement data based on metrics like accuracy, precision, linearity, and crosstalk. My data quality assessment is compatible with both 2D and 3D eye movement data.
I assess and compare the quality of data collected across a variety of platforms. 
### Deep Learning for Gaze-Based Biometric Authentication
Python, PyTorch. See our [publication](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/9304859) for a deep dive into eye movement biometrics.
